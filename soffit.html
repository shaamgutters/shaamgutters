Ask about your project →
What is the difference between soffit and fascia?

Soffit covers the underside of the roof overhang. Fascia runs along the outer roof edge. They meet at the eaves but serve different parts of the roofline.

Do I need vented or solid soffit?

The choice depends on the location and ventilation design. We review the application instead of assuming every panel should use the same vent pattern.

Will new soffit fix attic moisture or ice buildup?

Not by itself in every situation. Attic conditions involve several parts of a building. Tell us about the symptoms so we discuss assessment and the limits of the soffit work.

Do you coordinate soffit with siding and fascia?

Yes. We review the related finishes and installation sequence. Your estimate identifies the services included in the project.
