What Is It

Complete roofing systems built for Alberta

Your roof is your home's most critical layer of protection. Shaam Gutters installs asphalt shingle roofing systems designed for the specific demands of Alberta's climate — heavy snow loads, ice damming, hailstorms, and intense summer UV that ages inferior products quickly.

We handle full tear-offs, complete replacements, and targeted repairs. Every installation includes proper ice and water shield at the eaves, quality underlayment throughout, and manufacturer-certified installation that keeps your warranty intact.

  • Asphalt shingles — reliable, cost-effective, wide style range
  • 3-tab and architectural (laminate) shingle options available
  • Class 3 and Class 4 impact-rated shingles for hail-prone areas
  • Ice and water shield standard at all eaves and valleys
Why It Matters in Alberta

Alberta's roof is one of the hardest working in Canada

Alberta sits in one of North America's most active hail corridors — and a single large-hail event can total a roof. The province also experiences some of the heaviest snow loads of any region in Canada, combined with rapid melt events driven by chinook winds. These melt-refreeze cycles are the primary cause of ice dams, which force water back under shingles and into the structure.

Intense summer UV accelerates shingle granule loss, drying out the asphalt and cracking the mat underneath. A properly specified roof for Alberta — with the right impact rating, ice shield coverage, and ventilation — will outlast a standard-spec roof by a decade or more in this climate.

  • Alberta is in Canada's most active hail corridor — impact ratings matter
  • Snow loads up to 2.4 kPa design load require proper structural support
  • Chinook melt-refreeze cycles create ice dams without proper ice shield coverage
  • UV index across Alberta is among the highest on the prairies
Key Benefits

What a proper Alberta roof delivers

Hail & Impact Resistance

Class 3 and Class 4 impact-rated shingles are available. Class 4 is the highest rating and may qualify your home for insurance discounts in Alberta.

Ice Dam Protection

Ice and water shield is installed at every eave and valley — providing a waterproof membrane that stops meltwater from entering even when ice forces it back under the shingles.

Manufacturer Warranty

We install products in strict accordance with manufacturer specifications — ensuring the full material warranty is valid and protecting your investment for decades.

How It Works

Our roofing process

01

Full Inspection

We assess your current roof system — shingles, decking, flashings, valleys, and ventilation. You get a written report and a clear, fixed-price quote before anything begins.

02

Tear-Off & Disposal

All existing shingles and underlayment are removed, decking is inspected for soft spots or rot, and any problem areas in the deck are replaced before anything new goes on.

03

Ice Shield & Underlayment

Ice and water shield is applied at all eaves and valleys. Quality synthetic underlayment covers the full deck — the foundation of a roof that performs in Alberta's climate.

04

Install & Inspect

New shingles are installed per manufacturer specs. We conduct a full inspection before cleanup and walk you through the finished work.
