Understand the scope

More than a new cover on the roof edge.

Fascia trim and the material behind the trim are different parts of the job. A new aluminum cover does not repair damaged wood beneath. We discuss the condition and any additional replacement scope before covering the roof edge.

Underlying-condition review

Roof-edge length, height, profile, access and gutter removal affect price. The condition of the underlying board also matters. Ask about your project →
Does aluminum fascia replace the board behind the trim?

Not automatically. Aluminum covering and the supporting board are separate items. We review damage and quote supporting-material replacement where needed.

Should fascia work happen before new gutters?

We plan the sequence together. Where fascia needs replacement, completing the agreed supporting work before fitting new gutters often avoids removing new work again.

Do you match existing exterior colours?

We review available colours and the existing finish. Age, fading and product availability affect how closely new material matches.

Do you install fascia on new construction?

Yes. Send the plans or measurements, project location and preferred sequence. We confirm the fascia scope alongside any gutter or soffit work.
