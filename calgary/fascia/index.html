Before installation

More than a new cover on the roof edge.

We review the fascia and gutter connection together. Your scope should explain access, removal, supporting-material work and the finished aluminum. We do not cover a visible concern without discussing the next step with you.

What affects the cost?

Roof-edge length, height, profile, access and gutter removal affect price. The condition of the underlying board also matters. We identify supporting-material replacement separately from aluminum trim work.

Ask about your project →
What should I send for a Calgary fascia estimate?

Send your property address, photos of the roof edge, approximate fascia lengths and the number of storeys. Include preferred colours, access restrictions and whether you want to retain or replace your gutters. Photos help us review your request. We confirm the supporting-board condition and final scope during the assessment.

Do you replace fascia before new Calgary eavestroughs?

We review both services together. Where fascia or its supporting board needs work, we plan the sequence before installing the new gutter system.

Does aluminum fascia replace the board behind the trim?

Not automatically. Aluminum covering and the supporting board are separate items. We review damage and quote supporting-material replacement where needed.

Do you match existing exterior colours?

We review available colours and the existing finish. Age, fading and product availability affect how closely new material matches.

Do you install fascia on new construction?

Yes. Send the plans or measurements, project location and preferred sequence. We confirm the fascia scope alongside any gutter or soffit work.
