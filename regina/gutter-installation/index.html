The service details

What goes into your project.

01

5-inch & 6-inch options

We review gutter size alongside the roof layout, drainage outlets and your project requirements. Bigger is not automatically the right choice for every roof edge.

02

A connected drainage system

Plan outlets, downspouts and extensions with the gutter runs. Tell us about existing drainage connections, walkways and locations where water collects.

03

A written replacement scope

Confirm removal, disposal, colour, guards and any fascia work in the estimate. No assumption about an add-on should replace an agreed scope.

Before installation

Continuous runs. Attention at every connection.

Seamless gutters use continuous lengths along each run. Corners, end caps and outlets still need connections and sealing. We measure the runs, plan their support and complete the downspout layout as part of the installation.

What affects the cost?

Length, number of storeys, roof access, corners, downspout count, gutter size, guard options and removal all affect the estimate. Existing fascia damage adds a separate decision. We discuss additional work before proceeding.

Ask about your project →
Do you replace gutters for Regina homeowners?

Yes. We quote gutter replacement for homes and garages directly. Send your address, roofline photos and the sections causing concern. We review gutter size, downspout locations, removal and any fascia work before confirming your written estimate.

Do you quote Regina gutter subcontract work?

Yes. Email your plans, takeoff or site details, along with the requested gutter size and scope. We review the project and confirm pricing, documentation and availability directly.

Do you install both 5-inch and 6-inch gutters?

Yes. We offer 5-inch and 6-inch gutters. We review the roof layout and drainage requirements before recommending the project specification.

Are eavestroughs and gutters the same service?

Yes. We use both terms for the troughs along the roof edge. Your estimate also identifies downspouts and any other included drainage components.

Are gutter guards included?

We discuss guards as an option and identify them in the estimate. Guard type, maintenance needs and the condition of the existing system affect the choice.

What happens when you find damaged fascia?

We explain the concern and review the additional scope with you. Covering damaged wood with aluminum does not replace the need to assess the supporting material.
