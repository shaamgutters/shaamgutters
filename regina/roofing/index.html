Before installation

Roofing planned with the rest of your exterior.

A replacement roof often affects the work around the eaves. We review the roofing scope alongside gutter and trim requirements, identify the agreed materials and discuss access for loading, debris and cleanup.

What affects the cost?

Roof area, slope, height, number of existing layers, access, shingle selection, flashing and decking condition affect price. A project-specific assessment provides a better basis than a roof price without a defined scope.

Ask about your project →
Do you coordinate a Regina roof project with a property manager?

Yes. Provide the buildings, roof system, access details and decision-making contact. We confirm the scope and schedule after reviewing the project.

What type of roofing do you offer?

We quote asphalt shingle installation and replacement. For other roof systems, describe the project first so we confirm whether the work fits our scope.

Do you install impact-rated shingles?

Discuss the available products during your estimate. Any impact rating applies to the selected shingle and its test standard, not every roof we install. A product rating is not a promise of no future damage.

Are gutters included with a roof replacement?

Only when the written scope includes them. We also quote coordinated gutter, soffit and fascia work where the project requires those services.

What warranty applies to my roof?

Ask for the manufacturer and workmanship terms with your estimate. Coverage depends on the product, installation details and written agreement. We do not substitute a general website promise for those terms.
