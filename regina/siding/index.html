Before installation

A consistent finish from the wall to the roofline.

Small details influence a siding project: corner pieces, the finish around openings and how new panels meet existing materials. We review those transitions with you and identify any related soffit or fascia work in the estimate.

What affects the cost?

Wall area, height, product choice, removal, access, number of openings and preparation affect the estimate. Partial replacement also depends on the match with existing siding. We review additional work separately if removal reveals damage.

Ask about your project →
Do you coordinate Regina siding with soffit and fascia?

Yes. We review the finishes and installation order together. Your written estimate identifies the siding, soffit, fascia and gutter work included in the project.

What siding material do you install?

Vinyl siding is our main siding offering. Send details for any different material so we confirm the project scope before preparing an estimate.

Do you replace a single wall or elevation?

We assess partial replacements as well as larger projects. Product availability, colour matching and transitions to existing siding affect the recommendation.

Is insulation or sheathing included?

Only when the written estimate includes those items. We review preparation and concealed damage separately rather than assuming panel replacement covers every wall component.

Should I replace soffit and fascia at the same time?

The decision depends on their condition and your project goals. Coordinating colour and access is useful, but we quote related work as part of an agreed scope.
