Subcontract enquiries

A clear handoff from your team to ours.

SHAAM GUTTERS works with roofing contractors and builders, including ongoing gutter subcontract work in Regina. We quote full-home installations, new construction, townhomes and multi-building projects.

Our service scope includes 5-inch and 6-inch gutter installation, soffit, fascia, siding and asphalt shingle roofing. For managed properties, ask about planned gutter cleaning and clearly defined replacement work.

We confirm the services, site responsibilities and current availability after reviewing your project. Ask about insurance, applicable WCB documentation and site-specific requirements before onboarding.

Send your project details

  • Project address and site contact
  • Plans, measurements or building list
  • Required service, materials and colours
  • Requested sequence and access requirements
  • Who supplies materials and handles disposal
