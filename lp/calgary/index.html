A few useful answers.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. Estimates are free. Send your Calgary-area address and the service you need through the form above, or call 403-630-9192. We review the scope, materials and options with you before anything is scheduled.

Are you licensed and insured in Calgary?

Yes. SHAAM GUTTERS LTD is licensed and insured for the exterior work we carry out in Calgary and the surrounding area.

What warranty do you offer?

Gutter installations come with a 4-year warranty. Confirm the coverage that applies to your project when you review your estimate.

How long does a typical installation take?

It depends on the size of the home, the services included and access to the property. Your estimate confirms the scope, and we agree on timing with you before the work is booked.

Do you work with builders and roofing companies?

Yes. We accept subcontract enquiries for gutters and exterior work. Send project plans, the required scope and site details so we can review pricing and availability.
