Meet SHAAM GUTTERS LTD

Exterior work. Clear communication. One team.

We provide gutter and exterior services for homeowners, builders, roofing contractors and property managers in Calgary and Regina.

Our company

From Calgary roots to Regina projects.

SHAAM GUTTERS LTD began serving customers from the Calgary area. Our work now includes Regina and Saskatchewan projects, including gutter subcontracting for roofing contractors.

Our services cover gutter installation and replacement, cleaning, roofing, siding, soffit and fascia. We work on residential and commercial properties, new builds and exterior renovations.

Contact Abdul and the SHAAM team to discuss a home project, a construction site or subcontract requirements. We review the work before confirming the scope, pricing and availability.

What to expect

Practical commitments, from start to finish.

01

A written scope

Confirm the service, materials, preparation and options before scheduling. Any additional work needs a separate conversation.

02

Coordinated services

Plan the connections between roof, gutter, siding, soffit and fascia work. One point of contact helps keep the agreed scope clear.

03

A project-specific handover

Discuss the completed work, maintenance information and applicable product and workmanship terms. Ask for your warranty details in writing.
