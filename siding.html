What Is It

Exterior cladding that works hard

Siding is the exterior cladding that covers your home's walls — it's the first line of defence against wind, rain, hail, UV radiation, and temperature extremes. Shaam Gutters installs vinyl, fibre cement, and steel siding depending on your home's needs, your budget, and the look you're after.

Vinyl siding is low-maintenance and cost-effective. Fibre cement offers superior impact resistance and a painted, wood-look finish that stands up to hail. Steel siding provides the highest durability in Alberta's climate — Class 4 impact-rated options are available for maximum hail protection.

  • Vinyl siding — affordable, low-maintenance, wide colour range
  • Fibre cement — impact-resistant, paintable, premium look
  • Steel siding — maximum durability, Class 4 hail rating options
  • Proper housewrap and moisture barrier installation included

Why It Matters in Alberta

Alberta's weather demands tough siding

Calgary sits in one of the most active hail corridors in North America. A single large-hail event can puncture vinyl siding, crack fibre cement, and leave your home vulnerable to water infiltration behind the cladding. Choosing Class 4 impact-rated siding — and having it installed properly — is one of the best decisions an Alberta homeowner can make for long-term protection.

Beyond hail, Alberta's chinook winds drive rain horizontally against your walls with significant force. Poorly installed siding — missing laps, unfastened edges, inadequate housewrap — allows water to get behind the cladding and into your wall assembly, causing mould, rot, and insulation failure that's expensive to remediate. Proper installation is as important as the material itself.

  • Class 4 impact-rated options for maximum hail protection
  • Chinook winds drive rain at high force — installation quality matters
  • Extreme cold (-30°C+) can crack brittle or poorly installed panels
  • New siding also improves thermal performance and reduces heating costs
Key Benefits

What new siding does for your home

Hail Protection

Class 4 impact-rated siding absorbs hail strikes without cracking or puncturing — protecting your walls and potentially qualifying your home for insurance discounts.

Thermal Efficiency

New siding installed over insulated housewrap tightens your building envelope, reducing heat loss in winter and keeping your home cooler in summer.

Low Maintenance

Modern vinyl and steel siding holds its colour for decades and requires nothing more than an occasional rinse. No scraping, no priming, no annual painting.

How It Works

Our siding installation process

01

Inspect & Measure

We inspect your existing siding and wall sheathing, measure all wall areas, and recommend the right product for your home, budget, and exposure level.

02

Remove & Prep

Old siding is removed and the wall sheathing is inspected for moisture damage. Any problem areas are identified and addressed before new cladding goes on.

03

Housewrap & Barrier

A proper moisture barrier and housewrap is installed over the entire wall — a critical step that is often skipped but essential for long-term performance in Alberta's climate.

04

Install, Trim & Finish

New siding panels are installed from the bottom up, trimmed neatly at all windows, doors, and corners, and sealed at all penetrations before final cleanup.
